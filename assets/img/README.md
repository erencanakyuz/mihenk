## Contributor contract

- Put source images in this directory; do not embed base64 payloads in JavaScript.
- Use lowercase descriptive filenames and one of `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, or `.avif`.
- Keep post media at 16:9 where practical and provide honest alt text in `data/seed.js`.
- Do not add generated videos or GIFs here. Selected presentation media belongs in `assets/demos/`.
